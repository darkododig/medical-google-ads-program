Getting Started

How It Works

Remarketing setup is fast — your audiences are already being built by your existing Google Ads tracking. We turn that data into active campaigns within 1–2 weeks.

01
Audience Segmentation
Week 1
We review your existing Google Ads audience data and segment visitors by behavior — all site visitors, service-page visitors, booking-started but not completed, and time-based recency windows. Each segment gets its own targeting strategy and messaging approach.
02
Creative & Campaign Build
Week 1–2
We design responsive display ads in all standard sizes (including Google's responsive ad format) using your practice branding. Campaigns are configured with audience lists, bid strategies, frequency caps, placement exclusions, and geographic targeting aligned with your service area.
03
Launch & Monitor
Week 2+
Remarketing campaigns go live. We monitor impression share, click-through rates, conversion activity, and placement quality in the first 30 days — removing low-quality placements and adjusting bids as Google's algorithm learns your audience.
04
Ongoing Management
Included
Remarketing optimization is included in your Google Ads monthly management — no additional fee. Your account manager monitors performance, refreshes ad creative when needed, refines audience lists, and includes remarketing results in your monthly report.

Program Notes

Important Details

Before You Get Started

Google Ads is required. Remarketing management is included at no additional monthly fee within your active Google Ads program. It is not available as a standalone service — your Google Ads account, conversion tracking, and site visitor audiences are the foundation that makes remarketing possible and cost-effective.

Ad spend is separate. The $300 CAD setup fee covers Wisevu's time — it does not include your Google advertising budget. Remarketing ad spend is billed directly to your credit card by Google. We recommend a minimum of $150 CAD/month dedicated to remarketing for meaningful reach across your visitor audiences.

Setup takes 1–2 weeks. The setup fee covers audience list configuration, responsive display ad creative design, campaign build and configuration, frequency capping setup, placement exclusion setup, and initial bid strategy configuration.

Ongoing management is included. After setup, your remarketing campaigns are monitored, optimized, and reported within your existing Google Ads monthly management — no additional fee, no separate contract.
