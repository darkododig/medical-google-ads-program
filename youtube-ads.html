Getting Started

How It Works

From creative briefing to campaign launch — we handle the strategy, setup, and ongoing management so your video ads reach the right patients.

01
Strategy & Creative Brief
Week 1–2
We define your campaign objectives, target audience, and video ad format — skippable in-stream, non-skippable, or bumper ads. You receive a detailed creative brief outlining script structure, key messaging, and call-to-action recommendations for your video production team.
02
Campaign Build & Setup
Week 2–3
Once video creative is ready, we build your YouTube campaigns — configuring audience targeting (in-market, affinity, custom intent, remarketing), placement exclusions, bidding strategy, conversion tracking, and YouTube-specific ad settings for maximum reach efficiency.
03
Launch & Initial Optimization
Month 1
Campaigns go live. We monitor view rates, click-through rates, and conversion activity daily in the first 30 days — adjusting audience targeting, bid strategies, and placements to improve efficiency as the algorithm gathers data.
04
Optimize & Report
Ongoing
Monthly optimization cycles refine audience segments, test creative variations, and lower cost per booked patient. Your monthly report covers reach, qualified calls driven, website traffic, and leads generated — with a plain-English summary and next-month priorities.

Program Notes

Important Details

Before You Get Started

Video creative is sourced separately. Wisevu manages your YouTube campaigns — campaign strategy, targeting, bidding, optimization, and reporting. Video production is not included in the management fee. We provide a detailed creative brief to guide your production team, or can recommend production resources. Short-form video ad creative production is available as an add-on starting at $450 CAD.

Ad spend is separate. The monthly management fee covers Wisevu's time — it does not include your Google advertising budget. Ad spend is billed directly to your credit card by Google. We recommend a minimum of $300 CAD/month in ad spend to generate meaningful reach and conversion data.

Setup takes 2–3 weeks after video delivery. The $300 CAD one-time setup fee covers Google Ads account setup (if needed), campaign build, audience configuration, conversion tracking, and YouTube channel linking. Campaigns launch once video creative is received and approved.

Month-to-month with 30 days notice. Cancel anytime with 30 days written notice. Your Google Ads account, campaign history, audience lists, and video assets remain yours.

Landing Pages & CRO: Landing-page creation and conversion-rate optimization are not included in this program. We review your existing landing pages and may offer advisory recommendations for alignment with campaign intent, but dedicated landing-page design, development, or A/B testing are separate services.
