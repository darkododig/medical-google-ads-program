Important Details

Before You Get Started

Google Ads is required. Bing Ads management works alongside an active Google Ads program. We import and adapt your Google campaigns for Microsoft Advertising — this is how we deliver efficiency and speed at the $200/month price point.

Ad spend is separate. The monthly management fee covers Wisevu's time — it does not include your Microsoft Advertising budget. Ad spend is billed directly to your credit card by Microsoft. We recommend a minimum of $300 CAD/month in ad spend to generate meaningful data and results.

Setup takes 2–3 weeks. The $400 CAD one-time setup fee covers Microsoft Advertising account creation (if needed), campaign import and adaptation, conversion tracking configuration, and initial audience setup.

Month-to-month with 30 days notice. Cancel anytime with 30 days written notice. Your Microsoft Advertising account and all campaign data remain yours.
