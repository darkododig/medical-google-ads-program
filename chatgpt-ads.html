Phone Call & Form Tracking
$60 USD/month (vendor pass-through) + $300 CAD one-time setup

The base program includes GA4 form tracking for contact form and booking submissions. This add-on goes further — adding dynamic phone call tracking so you can attribute every inbound call to the specific campaign that generated it.

The add-on deploys dynamic number insertion (DNI): each visitor sees a unique tracking number based on their source. Calls are recorded, attributed, and fed back into campaign data to improve bidding and audience targeting toward phone leads.

The $60 USD/month fee is a direct pass-through to the call tracking platform and is billed in USD. Wisevu does not mark this up. The $300 CAD one-time setup covers DNI configuration, call recording setup, and GTM implementation.

CRM & Offline Conversion Tracking
Custom setup — quoted per integration

Standard conversion tracking captures online events — form submissions and call clicks. But the outcomes that matter most happen offline: a patient calls, qualifies, and books an appointment. Without connecting those outcomes, algorithms optimize for submissions — including spam and unqualified inquiries.

CRM and offline conversion tracking closes this gap. We connect your CRM or practice management system so that when a lead becomes a booked appointment, that outcome feeds back as a conversion. The platform then optimizes toward patients who book — not just patients who submit.

Compatible systems include HubSpot, Salesforce, Cliniko, Jane App, and most major EHR and booking platforms.

Setup complexity and cost vary by CRM and data structure. Contact us to discuss your booking workflow and we'll scope the integration.
