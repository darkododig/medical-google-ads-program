Phone Call & Form Tracking
$60 USD/month + $300 CAD one-time setup

For medical practices, a significant percentage of conversions happen over the phone — not via online forms. Without call tracking, you're missing a major portion of the actual ROI your campaigns are generating.

This add-on deploys dynamic number insertion (DNI) so each visitor sees a unique tracking number based on which campaign, keyword, or ad they came from. Calls are recorded, attributed to their source, and fed back into your Google Ads account so bidding algorithms can optimize for phone leads, not just clicks.

We also configure form submission tracking to capture every contact form, booking request, or callback submission with the same campaign-level attribution. Together, call and form tracking give you a complete picture of which keywords and campaigns are actually generating patients — not just website traffic.

The $300 CAD setup fee covers DNI configuration, call recording setup, Google Ads integration, and form tracking implementation via GTM.

Program Notes

Setup Fee: A one-time fee of $1,200 CAD covers comprehensive keyword research, campaign strategy, full campaign build, Google Tag Manager setup, GA4 integration, Google Ads conversion tracking, click fraud protection, and phone call tracking installation.

Management Fee: The management fee is $500 CAD/month or 10% of your monthly ad spend, whichever is greater. As your spend grows, the fee scales accordingly to reflect the additional work managing a larger account requires.

Ad Spend: Ad spend is separate from the management fee and is paid directly to Google by your practice. This keeps your spend fully transparent and in your control. We recommend a minimum of $600 CAD per month to generate meaningful data and results.

Account Ownership: All campaigns are built inside your own Google Ads account. You retain full admin access and ownership at all times — your account history, conversion data, and Quality Scores stay with you.

Commitment: Google Ads programs are month-to-month. A minimum 30-day written notice is required to cancel. There are no long-term contracts.

Pricing: Pricing is per practice. Multi-location practices are each treated as separate programs unless campaigns are structurally identical across locations — in which case discounts may apply.
